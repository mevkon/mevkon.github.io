Introduction

About me

Senior Product Designer working end-to-end — from early research and wireframing to the final, polished UI. I turn complex products into interfaces that feel simple and human.

I work best alongside engineers — a technical mindset lets me speak their language, so design decisions hold up in real products instead of staying in Figma. I lean on AI tools to move faster, and bring a strong background in visual storytelling and branding to everything I build.

I've always wanted to understand how things work — from spacecraft and the scale of the universe to the systems behind a good interface. That curiosity, paired with a love for new technology, is what made me a designer. It still drives me: taking something complicated and making it feel effortless.
