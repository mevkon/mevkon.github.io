Introduction

About me

Senior Product Designer focused on clear, user-centered, conversion-driven digital experiences built on design-thinking principles. I deliver products end-to-end — from early research and wireframing to the final, polished UI.

I specialize in transforming complex systems into simple, human-friendly interfaces that serve both user needs and business goals. I work closely with product managers and engineers in agile teams, and actively use AI tools and prompt engineering to accelerate design workflows. Alongside product work, I bring a strong background in visual storytelling and branding.
